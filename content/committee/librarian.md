The Librarian is responsible for sourcing, distributing, and organizing all sheet music for the orchestra throughout the year.

* **Course:** 4th Year Civil & Environmental Engineering
* **Instrument:** Violin
* **Favourite piece of music:** *Serenade for Strings* – Dag Wirén
* **Favourite thing about EUSO:** The wonderful people and close knit community we have!

Here are a few words from Issy:

> Hi everyone! I'm Issy, I play the violin and this will be my 4th year in EUSO. I'm super excited to be this year's librarian, sourcing and looking after all our music. Being in EUSO has easily been one of the best parts of uni - everyone is so lovely and the standard is really high! Looking forward to meeting and welcoming all the new members in September!
