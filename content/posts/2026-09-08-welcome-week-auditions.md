Welcome to the new academic year! EUSO has an exciting lineup of events for Welcome Week, followed by our annual auditions. 

Whether you're looking to join the orchestra or just want to hear us play, here is where you can find us:

---

### Tuesday 15 September

* **Activities Fair Stall**
  * **Location:** Bristo Square
  * **Time:** 10:00am – 4:00pm
  * Come by our stall to chat with current members, ask questions, and learn more about playing with EUSO!

* **Activities Fair Trio Performance**
  * **Location:** Bristo Square
  * **Time:** 11:00am

---

### Thursday 17 September

* **Auditions (Day 1)**
  * **Location:** Alison House, Lecture Theatre A
  * **Time:** 9:00am – 5:00pm

* **EUSO / EUCO Scratch Rehearsal**
  * **Location:** Reid Concert Hall
  * **Time:** 5:00pm – 9:00pm
  * Join us alongside the Edinburgh University Chamber Orchestra (EUCO) for a relaxed, open scratch rehearsal.

---

### Friday 18 September

* **Auditions (Day 2)**
  * **Location:** Alison House, Lecture Theatre A
  * **Time:** 9:00am – 5:00pm

---

### Audition Preparation
Please prepare a short solo piece or movement of your choice (approx. 3–5 minutes). We welcome string players from all faculties and degree disciplines, as well as non-students.

For more information on joining, check our [Join Us](/join/) page or reach out via our [Contact Page](/contact/).
