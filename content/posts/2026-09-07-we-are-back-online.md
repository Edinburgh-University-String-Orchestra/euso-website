After a four-year hiatus, the official Edinburgh University String Orchestra website is finally back up and running!

We have refreshed our digital home just in time for the 2026/27 academic year. Going forward, you will be able to find all our latest updates, concert announcements, audition schedules, and outreach events right here.

### What's Ahead
* **Welcome Week:** Meet us at the Activities Fair and join our scratch rehearsal.
* **Auditions:** Taking place on 17th and 18th September—check out the [Join Us](/join/) page for the sign-up link.
* **Concerts:** Keep an eye on our [Concerts](/concerts/) page as we announce our repertoire and performance dates for the upcoming semester.

Thank you for your patience while we tuned up behind the scenes. We are thrilled to be back and cannot wait to share our music with you this year!
